NAME
ToneLoom — AI Writer & Cross-LLM Context Assistant

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max ~132 characters — use this in “Summary”)
Weave your voice and context into every prompt. Learns your tone, keeps memory, and works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini & the web.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION (paste into “Description”)
ToneLoom is the personal writing layer for your browser. It learns your unique writing style, remembers key context from your emails and docs, and carries your conversations seamlessly across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and DeepSeek.

Select any text on a webpage and instantly:
• Rewrite in your voice (or Professional, Casual, Concise, and more)
• Summarize long content into clear points
• Expand short notes into full paragraphs
• Train ToneLoom on your writing so it learns habits like tone and abbreviations

Built for real workflows:
• Floating toolbar on Gmail, Notion, docs, and across the web
• Side panel for memory, switch (cross-LLM handoff), and settings
• Cross-LLM handoff — continue a chat from ChatGPT to Claude, Gemini, or DeepSeek without losing context
• Personal style learning with “My style” rewrites
• Domain controls — disable the toolbar on banking or work sites you choose

Plans:
• Free — core rewrites, 1 tone profile, basic memory starter
• Pro ($12/mo or $79/yr) — unlimited rewrites, cross-LLM handoff, up to 3 tone profiles
• BYOK ($4/mo or $29/yr) — bring your own OpenAI / Anthropic / Gemini API key

Install ToneLoom and write like yourself, everywhere you type.
