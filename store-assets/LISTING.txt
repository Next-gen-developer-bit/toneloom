NAME
EchoWrite — AI Writing Assistant

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max ~132 characters — use this in “Summary”)
Rewrite, summarize, and expand in your own voice. Learns your tone, keeps memory, and works across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini & the web.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION (paste into “Description”)
EchoWrite is a personalised AI writing assistant for Chrome.

Select any text on a webpage and instantly:
• Rewrite in your voice (or Professional, Casual, Concise, and more)
• Summarize long content into clear points
• Expand short notes into full paragraphs
• Train EchoWrite on your writing so it learns habits like tone and abbreviations

Built for real workflows:
• Floating toolbar on Gmail, Notion, docs, and across the web
• Side panel for memory, tone profiles, and settings
• Cross-LLM handoff — continue a chat from ChatGPT to Claude, Gemini, or DeepSeek without losing context
• Personal style learning with “My style” rewrites

Plans:
• Free — get started with core writing tools
• Pro ($9/mo) — unlimited rewrites, memories, tone profiles, and cross-LLM handoff

Install EchoWrite and write like yourself, everywhere you type.
